## Laptop and study rules

Customers may use laptops in the café.

During busy hours from 12:00 to 14:00, tables may only be used for 90 minutes.

Power outlets are available near the window seats.

Customers should use headphones when listening to music or watching videos.

---

## Lost and found

Lost items are kept for 30 days.

Phones, wallets, keys and documents are stored securely behind the counter.

Customers should describe the lost item before staff return it.

Food and drinks left behind are thrown away on the same day.

After 30 days, unclaimed items may be donated or disposed of.

---

## Pets and service animals

Small pets are allowed in the outdoor seating area.

Pets are not allowed inside the café.

Guide dogs and other trained service animals are allowed inside the café.

Customers are responsible for keeping their pets under control.

---

## Allergies and special diets

The café uses milk, eggs, wheat, soy and nuts in the kitchen.

The café cannot guarantee that any product is completely free from allergens.

Customers with serious allergies should tell a staff member before ordering.

Some drinks can be prepared with plant-based milk.

---

## Birthday cakes and outside food

Customers may bring a birthday cake for a small celebration.

Other outside food and drinks are not allowed.

Large birthday groups should contact the café in advance.

The café does not provide decorations for private celebrations.

---

## Complaints and incorrect orders

If an order is incorrect, customers should tell a staff member as soon as possible.

The café may replace an incorrect drink or food item.

Refunds are not guaranteed for products that have already been consumed.

Serious complaints should be passed to a staff member or manager.

---

## Table reservations

Small groups do not need a reservation.

Groups of six or more should contact the café in advance.

Reservations may be held for 15 minutes after the booked time.

The café cannot guarantee a specific table.

---

## Wi-Fi

Free Wi-Fi is available for customers.

The Wi-Fi password is printed on the receipt.

The café cannot guarantee uninterrupted internet access.

Customers should not use the Wi-Fi for illegal activities.
